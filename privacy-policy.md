# CookieNuke Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to CookieNuke. We operate the Chrome extension CookieNuke available at helixlabs.studio/products/cookie-nuke.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our extension. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the extension.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- Email address (when creating an account for paid plans)
- Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never store card details)
- Anthropic API key (for AI explanation features — stored locally only, never transmitted to HelixLabs)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- Browser type and version
- Subscription tier and authentication token

### 2.3 Browsing Data (Stored Locally Only)
CookieNuke processes network requests to identify trackers and detects cookie consent banners on pages you visit. This includes:

- Domain names of third-party trackers detected on each page
- Whether a cookie banner was detected and handled
- Privacy risk score per site

**Important:** All browsing data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to HelixLabs servers.

### 2.4 AI Explanation Features (BYOK Model)
CookieNuke uses a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model for AI features:

- Your Anthropic API key is stored locally in your browser and is never transmitted to HelixLabs servers
- When you use AI explanation features, tracker data is sent directly from your browser to Anthropic's Claude API using your own API key
- This data is not routed through or stored on HelixLabs servers
- We do not have access to your API keys or your AI conversations at any time

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your account (for paid plans)
- Process payments and manage subscriptions
- Verify subscription status to unlock premium features
- Send transactional emails (receipts, account updates)
- Respond to customer support requests
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

- **Stripe** — for payment processing
- **Anthropic** — only when you choose to use AI features with your own API key (direct connection from your browser)
- **Vercel** — for backend authentication services
- Law enforcement when required by law

## 5. Data Retention

- Browsing and tracker data: Stored locally in your browser. You can clear it anytime by signing out or uninstalling the extension
- Account data: Retained for as long as your account is active
- Deleted accounts: Personal data deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to processing of your data
- Data portability

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@helixlabs.studio

## 7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

- All browsing and tracker data is stored locally and managed by Chrome
- API keys are stored locally using Chrome's secure storage API
- Payment processing handled by PCI-compliant Stripe
- Authentication tokens are securely managed

## 8. Third-Party Services

### Anthropic Claude API
When you enable AI explanation features and provide your own Anthropic API key, tracker data is sent directly to Anthropic's services. Please review [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy) for information on how they handle data.

### Stripe
Payment processing is handled by Stripe. Please review [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) for information on how they handle payment data.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our extension is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

## 11. Your Choices

You can control your data in the following ways:

- **Remove API key:** Delete your Anthropic API key from extension settings at any time
- **Manage whitelist:** Add or remove trusted sites from extension settings
- **Sign out:** Remove your authentication token from the extension
- **Uninstall:** Remove the extension to stop all data collection
- **Delete account:** Contact us to delete your account and associated data
